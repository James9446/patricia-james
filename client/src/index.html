Chapel of Our Lady

Join us for our ceremony at the Chapel of Our Lady, a historic gem located in the heart of the Presidio. Built in 1864, this non-denominational chapel features hand-painted exposed beams and a soaring arched glass wall behind the altar. Best of all, it is located just steps away from the Officers' Club, where the reception will immediately follow.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I need to stay?

You can stay any number of nights between June 19–24. Choose your own check-in and check-out dates — there's no minimum stay required.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes, you can cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before your arrival date. Late cancellations will incur one night's room rate plus tax.

What if I book after May 20?

The discounted $139/night rate may no longer be available after the cutoff date. Standard rates during this period are $300–$500+ per night due to the World Cup.

What if the room block sells out?

Let me know! I may be able to add extra rooms (but with the World Cup it might be tricky). So book soon! I arranged the hotel block with Jimmy Legg — he's offered to be available to guests for any needs or concerns. You can contact him at jimmy.legg@ohrllc.com.

Is parking available?

Yes, valet parking is available on-site for an additional fee. The area is also very Waymo-friendly if you prefer self-driving taxis!

Is breakfast included?

Continental breakfast has historically been included, but please verify at time of booking as this may be subject to change.
